## 5. Jurisdiction notes

### India

- **Status.** Crypto-assets (termed "Virtual Digital Assets", VDAs) are legal to
  hold and transfer in India, but they are **not legal tender** and are not
  backed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The RBI has repeatedly cautioned
  the public about the risks of crypto-assets.
- **Taxation.** Gains from transferring a VDA are taxed under **Section 115BBH**
  of the Income-tax Act, 1961, at a flat **30%** (plus applicable surcharge and
  cess), with no deduction for expenses other than the cost of acquisition, and
  **losses from VDA transfers cannot be set off** against other income or
  carried forward. A **1% tax deducted at source (TDS)** applies on certain VDA
  transfers under **Section 194S**, with thresholds that differ for specified
  and non-specified persons. Reporting in the ITR is required.
- **AML / PMLA.** Virtual Digital Asset Service Providers (VASPs) are reporting
  entities under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act and must register with
  the **Financial Intelligence Unit – India (FIU-IND)**. This software is a
  personal tool, not a VASP service, but if you operate it on behalf of others
  you may fall within those obligations.
- **Tax and legal compliance is on you.** Consult a qualified chartered
  accountant and, if needed, a lawyer. Tax treatment can change between
  financial years — confirm the current rules.

### United States

- Crypto-assets are regulated across multiple agencies (SEC, CFTC, FinCEN, IRS,
  state regulators). This software does **not** register anyone as an investment
  adviser, broker-dealer, commodity trading adviser, or money transmitter, and
  is not intended for use in a manner that would require such registration.
- Automated trading may trigger tax reporting; US persons must report crypto
  gains/losses to the IRS.
- You must comply with **OFAC sanctions** and applicable export controls. Do not
  use this software to transact with sanctioned persons or jurisdictions.

### European Union

- The Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (**MiCA**, Regulation (EU) 2023/1114)
  governs crypto-asset service providers (CASPs). This software is not a CASP
  service. Operating it commercially for third parties may bring you within MiCA
  and national licensing regimes.
- GDPR applies to any personal data you process while using it.

### United Kingdom

- Crypto-assets are high-risk and largely unregulated as investments. The
  Financial Conduct Authority (**FCA**) rules on financial promotions apply to
  marketing crypto-assets to UK consumers. Automated trading may have tax
  implications (HMRC).

### Other major markets

- **Singapore** — regulated by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) under
  the Payment Services Act; **UAE** — Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority
  (VARA); **Australia** — ASIC; **Canada** — provincial securities regulators
  (CSA); **Japan** — Financial Services Agency. Each regime imposes licensing
  and/or reporting obligations on anyone providing services to others. As a
  personal tool you generally fall outside licensing, but confirm your own
  position.
